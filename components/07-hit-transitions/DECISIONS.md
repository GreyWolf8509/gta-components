# 07 Hit transitions: decisions

## Evidence (both variants)
- Top GTA editors cut with a small set of full-frame transitions: soft cross-dissolves with a milky veil (yt-b5XEkjldydc t00012), dips / holds on pure black (tt-7668382628088696086 t00034.5, tt-7579217066415099158 t00004.5, tt-7669519999714725141 t00075), a VHS colour-bar glitch flash (yt-gVyZUp6IWV4 t00345), an anime burst hit (yt-232CmcU0wKg t00643) and a curved-panel warp (yt-mCDH5KnBx9o t00052).
- Job: change shots with energy on a beat. Position: full frame, no text. Look: preset-grade. Dissolves grey the frame, dips are linear opacity fades, glitches are stock bars, nothing is motion-blurred by velocity.
- Motion: single-sample effects, 0.3 to 1 s, linear.

## Our upgrade (shared engine)
- One pure `render(t)` function drives every layer from timeline time (one Look.drive over the whole comp), so every frame is seek-safe and transitions never fight each other.
- Motion blur is computed from velocity: blur sigma = 0.3 x the per-frame displacement (SVG directional Gaussian), so the smear exists only while the image is actually moving fast and resolves to razor sharp.
- Zoom blur is a real radial blur: 10 time-aligned ghost copies of the shot, each scaled outward by the per-frame zoom delta (taps crowded toward 1x, k^1.5) and averaged (opacity 1/(i+2)), so displacement grows with radius: the centre reads, the edges streak. Outer taps are shaded and contrasted (no lift), so the edges tunnel instead of fogging. A barrel lens warp (feDisplacementMap off a precomputed r^2 map) stretches the edges through the cut.
- Light adds, never veils: exposure pulses are brightness/saturate on footage (blacks stay black); the flash hit adds a thresholded highlight bloom (screen) so light comes off the bright parts of the shot only.
- Dip to black is a luma dip: an SVG gamma curve crushes shadows first and lets highlights die last (and rise first on the way back in), like a film fade, not a linear opacity fade.
