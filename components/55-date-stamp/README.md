# 55 Date stamp

The centred date and time stamp creators drop on a "later that night" jump: a day or date line and a clock, mid-frame, no box. Built as real extruded 3D lettering (Three.js) with mechanical clock drums. Both variants are transparent-ready overlays.

- **A `a/` (16:9, long-form): Date and time.** "SATURDAY 7:59 PM / LOS SANTOS" types on behind a yellow caret and the colon ticks. On the cut, the clock flips to 8:00 as three drums carry, and the time locks into yellow metal with a light sweep. It clears with a short slanted wipe. Use it to open a scene or mark a jump to a time of day.
- **B `b/` (9:16, Shorts): Time jump.** "THREE HOURS LATER" over a big clock that fast-forwards 03:12 to 06:12 and stops with a click, locking into yellow metal. Use it on a Short when time skips.

## CONFIG (top of each `index.html`)
- **A:**
  - `day`;
  - `time.from` / `time.to` (H:MM or HH:MM, same digit count; each digit is a drum that rolls forward to `to`);
  - `time.suffix` ("PM", "AM" or "" for 24 h);
  - `place`;
  - `type` (sizes, max ink width, extrusion depth and bevel, block centre);
  - `colors` (white enamel, yellow state metal, caret);
  - `timing` (`type` start, `cps` per letter, colon `blinkOff`, `lock`, the `out` wipe, `end`).
- **B:**
  - `caption`;
  - `clock.from` / `clock.to` (24 h HH:MM; rolls forward and wraps past midnight);
  - `type`, `colors`;
  - `timing` (`roll` start and `rollDur`, `endSpeed` arrival speed before the click, `out`, `end`).

## Render
```
cd a && npx hyperframes render . --variables '{"bg":"none"}' --format webm -o overlay.webm   # transparent overlay
cd a && npx hyperframes render . -o out.mp4                                                 # over the sample GTA V footage
```
The same commands work in `b/`.

## Your own clip
Replace the `<video class="clip shot">` sources in `index.html` (keep `muted playsinline`, set `data-start` / `data-duration` / `data-media-start`), or render `bg:none` and lay the webm over your edit. In A, put your cut on `timing.lock`: the clock flips on it.

## Files
- `media/card3d.js`: the 3D lettering, drums, caret and post (bloom, motion blur, contact shadow).
- `media/glyphs.js`: Archivo outlines, regenerated by `media/gen-glyphs.js` (fontkit).
