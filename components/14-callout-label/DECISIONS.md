# 14 Callout label: decisions

## Variant A (16:9, "Leader callout")
**Evidence.** Creators pin a short, heavy label right on the thing (yt-J2ed1YF71ZU t00484 "150 mt": heavy rounded white sans, thin black outline + drop shadow, ~10% of frame height, tilted with the floor; tt-7578844325090561287 "2025": heavy rounded yellow, outline + shadow; yt-mCDH5KnBx9o t00116: white arrows pointing at the thing, a label beside it; yt-o4nNjj44d4M t00267: a tilted neon/rubber stamp that slams in). No boxes; legibility from outline and shadow. Motion: pops / stamps, sometimes tracked.

**Our upgrade.**
- The label is real geometry: Inter 900 outlines traced from the font, extruded 30 px with a 4.5 px rounded bevel, tilted top-back so the graded metal sides show; white enamel face (#FFFFFF to #D9E0E8), one uniform 6 px ink outline around face + extrusion and a soft contact shadow, in post, so it keeps the caption vernacular.
- The pin is a clearcoat bead with a red metal collar; two ping rings on landing. The leader is a white core over an ink stroke with a drop shadow; it draws from the bead rim to the elbow, then the shelf runs out under the label.
- The label rises out of its own shelf through a clipped window, lands with a 7% squash, and rides the car on a damped spring (tracked with LK optical flow, media/track.py), so the leader flexes as the car moves.
- **State change (payoff):** on the cut to the rare car the pin re-lands, the leader re-draws, the spring glides the label across (yaw and lean from its velocity), and the price rolls on a real 3D drum through the window ($90K to $4.7M, the Deluxo's Warstock price). A light pulse runs pin to elbow to shelf, then a feathered light front turns the enamel into polished gold with a dark reflection horizon, a raking key light, HDR bloom (3-frame peak) and a gold ping. A glossy red RARE CAR stamp slams on (1.75x to 1x, -11 to -3 deg, thud). One soft specular sweep at 2.85 s.
- Exit: label sinks back into the shelf, shelf and leader retract into the pin, the pin pops; nothing fades.

**Shots.** v57 gp red car (0-2.03, the "stock" car, HUD is real gameplay), v17 Deluxo at sunset (2.03-3.63, the rare car; it lifts off and the label rides up), v07 biplane sunset tail (0.37 s).
**Payoff:** 2.27 s ignite, hero 2.9 s.

## Variant B (9:16, "Stacked callouts, one locks in")
**Evidence.** Same vernacular in Shorts: heavy white or yellow caption type on the subject, outline + shadow, sized ~4% cap height; labels appear one at a time (mCDH5KnBx9o t00112-t00116).

**Our upgrade.** Same 3D label system. **Footage (r08):** every shot at native 1.0x in the Shorts blur-fill layout: a 1080x1080 window of each 16:9 master at y 440 over a blurred, darkened copy of the same shot (`b/media/plate-v.mp4`, built by `b/media/make_plate.sh`; no upscale, no sharpening). 0-2.40 s: v49-h (Franklin past the Families mural, window x 480-1560, no HUD); hard cut at 2.40 s to v06-h (sunny Franklin and Lamar close shot, window x 640-1720) for the payoff. Three callouts stack in the gameplay shot, staggered 0.10 / 0.28 / 0.46 s, tracked on the 1.0x masters (`media/tracks.js`: fr / og2 / fam2):
- FRANKLIN: pin on his hair crown; the leader angles up-right to a label above-right of his head.
- OG: pin on the white vest; the label **hangs** under the pin from an overline, so the leader ends above the type.
- FAMILIES: green pin on the BMX rider's back; the leader runs up clear of his head to a label above the crew.

Leaders and shelves are bold enamel tubes (18 px ink keyline, 9 px white core, shaded underside, offset shadow); every rail ends in two 10 px caps in its pin's colour. Small landing rings (38/28 px on a head, 58/44 elsewhere) so no ring crosses a face. OG and FAMILIES retract with label, rail and leader together (no leader stub) at 2.12 / 2.20.

**Payoff (lands on the cut to the sharp Franklin shot): FRANKLIN locks in.**
- 2.40 s cut: the FRANKLIN pin re-lands on Franklin's hair crown in v06 (small, springs up with a squash); the leader stays attached the whole time and the label glides to its lock slot (centre x 540, baseline 420), banking only slightly.
- 2.44 s a light pulse runs up the leader and out along the rail, charging it green; the collar turns from caption yellow to Franklin green (no flare: the bead is dimmed against the key-light boost, so no light lands on his face).
- 2.48 s the deep green lacquer plaque (Three.js slab, chamfered, bevelled, Franklin-green metal rim, the type's cast shadow) hinges up behind the word and locks at ~2.60 s with a 1.06 punch, a 3-frame rim flash and a bloom peak on the plaque; 2.52 s a light front ignites the type from white enamel to green metal; one specular rake at 3.25 s.
- Locked hold to 3.82 s with a 150-240 px leader following his head; exit (type sinks, plaque folds, rail and leader retract, pin pops) is clean by 3.97 s; end 4.033 s (the last v06 frame). Hero 3.15 s.
