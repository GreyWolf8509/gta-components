# 75 Honor to profile slider: decisions

- Instanced 2026-11-21 from the film kit object in films/gta6-rdr2-gta5 (assets/kit/film.js, `slider`), over the official footage the film uses under it. Starting point for the gauntlet; the film's version is flat DOM and must be rebuilt with material, light and a real payoff.

## Evidence
No census frame of its own (a film object derived from the comparison teardown). Nearest census objects: `65-scale-slider` (the GTA VI VIOLENCE choice slider: track, glowing knob, caps stop labels, the knob slides between stops, 3.8M views), `64-bond-meter` (pink relationship bar with two end badges, bottom centre) and `26-transformation-meter` (fill meters with a hero end). Creators' versions are flat vector UI: a 1 px track, a flat fill, a white dot, small caps labels, no light. The job: a meter whose identity changes, used as a comparison object in a long-form thesis video (RDR2 honor -> GTA VI criminal profile) and as a Shorts beat.

## Variant A (16:9, 1920x1080, 6.6 s)
**Our upgrade.** The meter is a physical object, not DOM lines:
- a Three.js housing: a navy machined slab (1320x184, 26 deep, 5 px bevel; key-lit gradient face texture, anodised navy-grey metal sides with clear coat and a PMREM studio env) with a title tab welded on its top-left (same material, 0.5 px proud so it never z-fights);
- the track is a recessed slot: a lighter metal lip ring around a dark glossy channel; a shader plane on the channel draws the inner shadow from the top lip, a lit bottom edge and the liquid fill (vertical 3-stop material, a soft specular band at 0.42, a hot core, the top sitting in the lip's shadow);
- the knob is a bevelled ceramic puck (lathe: r 40, 26 tall, 7 px chamfer, clear coat) in a metal collar with an identity-coloured LED inset, casting a soft contact shadow on the track (radial shader plane that follows it);
- labels are DOM Oswald 600 (title 44 px, ends 42 px, 0.08-0.12 em) pinned to projected anchors on the housing face, so contrast against the navy face is >= 5:1 whatever the footage does; the active end carries the identity tint.

**Beats.** Housing enters 0-0.38 s (tilts up from -1.1 rad, scales 0.85 -> 1, canvas blur 8 -> 0); labels roll up through overflow windows 0.26-0.6 s; knob pops onto HIGH HONOR (home 0.9) at 0.42 with a landing squash while the oxblood fill sweeps 0 -> 0.9.
**Flip (2.3 s).** The identity change has thickness: a soft light sweep crosses the housing face left -> right (0.5 s), the fill and the knob LED swap to GTA VI pink exactly where the sweep front passes, title and end labels roll out/in through their windows (old fully out before new enters), the knob travels back to PROFESSIONAL (0.08) with a back-out settle, 3 px jolt.
**Payoff (slide 3.6 -> 4.2 s, on the cut to the shop shot).** Knob accelerates (power 2.4 ease-in) with a stretch and an inertia lean, slams into the end stop: 1-frame impact (shake 6, flash 0), squash 0.65 -> rebound, 0.02 overshoot/rebound on the track, the fill catches up, IRREDEEMABLE lights (3-frame brightness peak, localised to the label, scale 1.14 punch, settles pink), the knob turns pink with a lit LED, the hot core boosts. Hold to 5.85 s.
**Exit.** Labels roll down out of their windows (5.85-6.07), then the housing tips back and collapses with a 6 px blur (6.0-6.3), zero frame before 6.3; 0.3 s footage tail.
**Shots.** Official GTA VI footage already in a/assets/clips: gta6-t2-008 (Jason to the truck) 0-3.6 s, gta6-t2-013 (the shop) 3.533-6.6 underneath with a 2-frame overlap; neither clip requests its last frame.

## Round log
- r01: first build (above).
  Self-review before the critic: the first build's housing read as a flat navy card, so it became a brushed-metal bezel (extruded shape with a real hole) with the navy panel sunk 8 px into it; the puck stays white ceramic (a pink puck vanished on the pink fill) with a top groove ring and a calmer LED (0.9, flares to 2.4 at the hit); the title tab now pivots on its left edge and widens with the title; the flip sweep is a narrow diagonal rake (uW 46, 0.48) instead of a wide white band; label lines are hidden once out of their windows; the metal's env reflection ramps in with the entrance. Evidence sheet and blind pairs are built from the 65/64/26 census frames (this object has none of its own in the manifest).
- r01 verdict: v0 7, v1 7, one defect (v1 puck rim past the 9:16 safe edge at the IRREDEEMABLE stop). Biggest gap: the identity change was skin-deep (same navy housing for both games) and neither beat landed on a cut.
- r02 (both open):
  - **The object itself changes identity.** Two skins share the same geometry: the RDR2 state is worn leather (deterministic grain texture, brass-lit top edge, oiled clear coat) with a brass lip and brass collar and a parchment-cream fill (RDR2's honor bar is white); the GTA VI state is the brushed navy bezel, navy panel, navy-grey lip and pink fill. The swap runs along the flip's diagonal light sweep with renderer clipping planes (old skin kept ahead of the front, new skin behind it), so the housing, tab and panel turn into the other game as the rake crosses them; the fill, LED, lip and collar follow the same front.
  - **Beats on cuts.** The first clip starts at media 0.3 s so its internal cut (porch -> cab) lands at 2.5 s; the flip impact now fires at 2.5 s (flip 2.24). The slide starts on the shop cut (3.6) and slams at 4.0 (0.4 s travel).
