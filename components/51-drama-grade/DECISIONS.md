# 51 Drama grade: decisions

## Variant A (16:9 long-form): Black-and-white beat
**Evidence.** Top long-form creators (yt-gVyZUp6IWV4 t84, yt-ePQI2A8d6kQ t144, yt-J2ed1YF71ZU t88) hard-cut a gameplay moment to full black and white for a dramatic or sad beat, held 1-8 s, full frame, often with the facecam left in colour or also drained. Their grade is a flat desaturate with a little contrast (milky mids, lifted blacks), no transition design, no type. yt-c-I-jXpGuOk / yt-zvdxphGS07o show the "cinematic" end: teal-orange, halation, vignette, fringing.

**Our upgrade.**
- A real noir grade, not a desaturate: a 65^3 LUT (grade/noir.cube) with a red-filter luma mix (skin and the orange jacket glow), black point 0.045 crushed to true black, an S-curve (c 1.55), a faint warm highlight tint, plus baked halation (highlights > 0.6 blurred 26 px, peach tint, screen 0.5). Lamps bloom, blacks stay #000.
- The grade arrives on the hit as a 7-frame drain: a leaning lit seam (white core, warm halation on the graded side, cool fringe on the colour side, stretched while it travels) sweeps left to right with the graded frames behind it; a 9 px shake + 1.028 punch lands on it; 2.39:1 bars slam in (expo.out 0.34 s); grain (true overlay on the footage) and vignette rise.
- Look chip (our own machined tag: before/after disc that spins over with an elastic settle, "LOOK 01 | NOIR") lands in the top bar.
- Title line "HE NEVER CAME BACK" writes on char by char (Archivo 600, 118% wide, +0.26em, 66 px; each char snaps opaque in 2 frames and resolves from 9 px blur), then a rule draws out.
- Payoff, the next beat (4.5 s): colour floods back from the subject as an iris with a broad lit ring (warm band, hot edge, screen), the returning colour arrives hot (saturate 1.4) and settles; a small punch. Chip collapses into its disc and pops; title drops out of its window; bars retract.
- CONFIG.look picks the baked plate: noir / bleach / tealorange (all three baked in a/media, same frames and timestamps as the source).

**Shot.** v35 penthouse (one 9 s tracking shot, long hold with a 1.065 push, media 1.4-7.8). The party in B&W, colour returns on the pink dusk skyline and the orange jacket.

**Payoff.** 4.5-5.16 s colour flood; hero 4.62.

## Variant B (9:16 Short): Silhouette grade
**Evidence.** tt-7668382628088696086 (9.5 M): a colour dialogue shot hard-cuts at ~24 s into a high-contrast B&W silhouette grade (figures pure black, sky blown to white-grey, soft highlight glow), held across several beat-cut shots to the end. No graphics, no label. tt-7594612539128859935: warm bloom look in colour.

**Our upgrade (r07).**
- **The arrival is one bold beat on a cut**, as in the evidence: warm colour (v01, the lifeguard tower in the sun) hard-cuts at 0.667 s into the silhouette grade on the drive-by gunman (v14: cap, arm and palms go pure black against the sunset sky). The cut lands with a punch from 1.10 that decays with a 10 px shake (0.38 s), a contrast slam (contrast 1.42 settling over 9 frames, CSS filter only), the vignette closing in at 0.9 and settling, grain on.
- **Clean silhouettes, skies that keep their clouds:** a smoothed tone LUT with a crushed toe (luma < 0.33 pure black) and a narrow black-to-sky transition (0.44 -> 0.56), so even the defocused palms resolve in a few anti-aliased pixels (no grey ring), then a soft highlight shoulder so skies sit at 75-90% with their clouds (v14 sky p50 ~210, p99 ~229). Cloud-scale clarity only on open sky at least ~14 px from any figure; a light warm halation (0.20, 26 px) wraps the edges.
- **Held across beat cuts, with one slow push** through the grade (1.00 -> 1.06, power1.inOut, hit -> match cut): gunman (1.0 s) -> the shoes on the wire (0.8 s) -> the kept red.
- **One red kept, 1.23 s, across a match cut:** a car's tail light (v16, window x=350, toe 0.1 so the sunlit road crushes) glows as a crimson lamp on a black car against a white sky (0.93 s), then the cut lands on a second car's two tail lights in the same place (v17 in the grade, 0.3 s). The lamps are lights, not textures: a solid, 2 px choked, 1.8 px feathered key (per-shot hue/saturation/value key; the value floor keeps the dark sunset reflections on the chrome out), a smooth interior from the lamp's own luma plus a distance-field core (#E0141E body -> hot pink-white centre), a 60 px #FF3B30 bloom.
- **The red drives the snap (3.667 s):** the tail lights surge for 4 frames (bloom swells, red bleeds into the black), then the frame **snaps to full colour on the beat** on the same wide sunset shot: the tail lights flare, a soft localised light (gaussian only, no hard shape) starts on the left one, holds 3 frames, and sweeps across the rear while it decays (0.35 s); colour arrives hot (+0.30 saturation over 0.45 s); true black point. Punch 1.05 + 7 px shake.
- **Colour holds 1.23 s at 1.0x** (no push on the last shot; a slow baked reframe follows the car): the car lifts off over explosions. Ends on the shot's last frame (4.9 s).
- No label by default (`label: false`): creators never name the grade. The optional look tag stays in CONFIG.

**Shots.** v01 tower (colour) -> HIT -> v14 drive-by silhouette -> v24 shoes on the wire -> v16 tail light, red kept -> match cut -> v17 tail lights, red kept -> SNAP (same shot) -> colour. All wide or vehicle shots; no gp footage. Shots overlap 2 frames (incoming starts underneath); cuts checked on their frames (20, 49, 73, 101), snap on frame 110.

**Payoff.** Red kept 2.43-3.67 (hero 3.55), snap 3.667, colour hold to 4.9.
